# WoW Forever

*The ready check window on WoW Forever: its own buffs, enchants and group utility, in dungeons and raids.*

On **WoW Forever** the same window opens on a ready check, in dungeons and in 10, 20 and 40 player raids, with Forever's own checks: Mark of the Wild, Power Word: Fortitude, Arcane Intellect, Battle Shout and the paladin Blessings (the raid-wide versions count too), Well Fed, a flask or elixir, weapon oil or stone, and enchants on the neck, back, chest, wrists, hands, feet and weapons (a shield too). The group bar shows interrupts, dispels, battle res and Bloodlust from Forever's classes. There's no Mythic+ there, so scores and keys are hidden.

| Icon | Checks on WoW Forever |
|---|---|
| **Class buffs** | Mark of the Wild or Gift of the Wild, Power Word: Fortitude or Prayer of Fortitude, Arcane Intellect or Arcane Brilliance, Battle Shout, and any paladin Blessing. Each only shows when someone in the group can cast it |
| **Well Fed** | Any food buff |
| **Flask or elixir** | Any flask or elixir |
| **Weapon oil or stone** | Oils, sharpening stones and weightstones on your weapon. Only shown for you |
| **Enchants** | Missing enchants on the neck, back, chest, wrists, hands, feet, main hand, and off hand when it's a weapon or a shield |

The group bar works it out from each player's class: interrupts (Rogue, Warrior, Mage, Shaman, Paladin, Druid and the Warlock's Felhunter), magic dispel (Priest, Paladin, Warlock), curse dispel (Mage, Druid), poison and disease dispel, battle res (Druid, Warlock) and Bloodlust (Shaman). Talents can change some of these, so treat it as likely.

Raid mode works the same as on Retail: in a raid the list scrolls and only shows players with something to fix.
