# Group utility and keys

*Interrupts, dispels, battle res and Bloodlust for the whole group, and everyone's keystones.*

### The utility bar

Above the rows, one icon each for:

- **Interrupts**
- **Magic, curse, poison and disease dispels**
- **Battle res**
- **Bloodlust**

**Green** when your group has it, **amber** for a single interrupt, **red** when nobody brings it. Hover an icon for who brings it.

> It's worked out from each player's class and spec, so a talent choice can change what someone actually has.

### Keystones

The right end of the bar counts the keystones in your group. Hover it for each player's key, highest first.

- **Your own key** always shows.
- **Other players' keys** show when an addon that shares keys, such as BigWigs, is loaded. Party iLvl Check doesn't include one itself.
