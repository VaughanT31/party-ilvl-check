# Sharing with friends

*Group members who also run Party iLvl Check fill in your window much faster.*

When others in your group also run Party iLvl Check, they send you their own item level, spec, tier pieces and gear check straight away, so they don't need inspecting at all. Anyone one of you inspects is shared with the rest, so in a raid the group splits the inspecting instead of everyone inspecting everyone.

- A small Party iLvl Check icon sits beside the names of everyone who has the addon, and the top of the window says how many of you have it.
- Hover a player's gear icon to see where their data came from.
- Shared data is only used for people in your group, nobody can send data for someone else, and your own inspect results are never replaced.
- Nothing is sent during boss encounters or Mythic+ runs.
