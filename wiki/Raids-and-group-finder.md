# Raids and group finder

*Raid mode for big groups, and checking applicants when you list a key.*

### Raid mode

In a raid group the window lists everyone as compact rows in a scrolling list, with item level, spec, the buff and gear icons and the utility bar.

- It doesn't pop up by itself in raids. Open it with `/ilvlcheck` or the minimap button.
- Scans keep their results when people join, leave or move groups, so only the new people are inspected.
- `/ilvlcheck testraid` shows a made-up raid.

### Group finder applicants

When you list a Mythic+ key, each applicant's row shows their **best run in your dungeon**, and their item level turns **orange** when it's under your Min iLvl.
