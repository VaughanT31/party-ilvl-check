Know your group is ready before the first pull. Party iLvl Check opens the moment you zone into a dungeon and shows every player's item level, Mythic+ score, buffs, food, flask, enchants and gems in one window. Under-geared tank, no flask, Mark of the Wild missing? You'll see it before the pull, not after the wipe.

  • Item level and Mythic+ score for everyone, inspected for you.
  • Buff, food and flask check, green or red per player.
  • Enchant and gem check: missing enchants and empty sockets on everyone's gear.
  • Your own minimum item level, with a warning when someone is under it.

No setup and no manual inspecting. It stays out of the way once a Mythic+ timer is running.

Opens When You Need It
Enter a 5-player instance and the window opens and inspects your whole group through the game's own inspect data. Each row shows item level, how far over or under your minimum it is, Mythic+ score in the game's own score colours, spec and role. Players who were out of range are checked again automatically once they come close. It hides once a Mythic+ keystone timer starts, so it never gets in the way of a key.

New: Faster Together
When others in your group run Party iLvl Check too, they send you their own item level, spec and gear check the moment the window opens, so they never need inspecting. Anyone one of you inspects is shared with the rest, so a raid fills in far faster. Shared data only counts for people in your group, nobody can send it for someone else, and nothing is sent during encounters or Mythic+.

New: Resize the Window
Drag the corner grip to make the window smaller or bigger, or type /ilvlcheck scale 0.8. It remembers the size.

Their Best Run in This Dungeon
An overall score says little about the key you're about to run. Each row now shows that player's best run in this dungeon: +14 here in green when timed, in orange when only over time, or none here. Outside a dungeon it uses the dungeon of your own keystone.

New: Group Utility Check
A bar above the players shows what your group brings: interrupts (amber with only one), magic, curse, poison and disease dispels, battle res and Bloodlust. Green when covered, red when nobody has it. Hover an icon to see who brings it. Worked out from each player's class and spec, so talents can change it.

New: Everyone's Keys
The group bar counts the keystones in your group, and hovering it lists each player's key, highest first. Your own key always shows. Other players' keys come from addons that share keys, like BigWigs, so they show when those are installed.

New: Tier Set and Post to Chat
The gear icon now says how many tier set pieces each player wears. The Post button sends a short summary to party or instance chat: item levels, best runs here, what the group is missing and everyone's keys.

New: Raid Mode
In a raid group the window lists everyone as compact rows you can scroll, with item level, spec, buffs, enchants and the group utility bar. Open it with /ilvlcheck or the minimap button before a pull.

New: Check Applicants to Your Key
List a Mythic+ group and each applicant's row shows their best run in your dungeon at a glance, green when timed. Item levels under your Min iLvl turn orange.

Buffs, Food and Flasks
Each player gets a ring of icons, green when active and red when missing. Hover one to see which it is.

  • Class buffs: Mark of the Wild, Battle Shout, Arcane Intellect, Power Word: Fortitude, Blessing of the Bronze and Skyfury.
  • Well Fed: any Midnight food or feast, including the reduced Hearty buff from someone else's feast.
  • Flask: Midnight flasks.
  • Weapon oil: oils and whetstones on your own weapon (the game doesn't share other players' weapon enchants).

Enchants and Gems
An icon on each row flags missing enchants and empty sockets on that player's gear. Hover it to see exactly which slots.

Minimum Item Level
Type a number into Min iLvl and anyone under it is flagged with a raid warning and a sound. Summary tiles at the top count who is ready, who is below your minimum and who is offline.
