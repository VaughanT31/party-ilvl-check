Know your group is ready before the first pull. Party iLvl Check opens the moment you zone into a dungeon and shows every player's item level, Mythic+ score, buffs, food, flask, enchants and gems in one window. Under-geared tank, no flask, Mark of the Wild missing? You'll see it before the pull, not after the wipe.

  • Item level and Mythic+ score for everyone, inspected for you.
  • Buff, food and flask check, green or red per player.
  • Enchant and gem check: missing enchants and empty sockets on everyone's gear.
  • Your own minimum item level, with a warning when someone is under it.

No setup and no manual inspecting. It stays out of the way once a Mythic+ timer is running.

Opens When You Need It
Enter a 5-player instance and the window opens and inspects your whole group through the game's own inspect data. Each row shows item level, how far over or under your minimum it is, Mythic+ score in the game's own score colours, spec and role. Players who were out of range are checked again automatically once they come close. It hides once a Mythic+ keystone timer starts, so it never gets in the way of a key.

Buffs, Food and Flasks
Each player gets a ring of icons, green when active and red when missing. Hover one to see which it is.

  • Class buffs: Mark of the Wild, Battle Shout, Arcane Intellect, Power Word: Fortitude, Blessing of the Bronze and Skyfury.
  • Well Fed: any Midnight food or feast, including the reduced Hearty buff from someone else's feast.
  • Flask: Midnight flasks.
  • Weapon oil: oils and whetstones on your own weapon (the game doesn't share other players' weapon enchants).

Enchants and Gems
An icon on each row flags missing enchants and empty sockets on that player's gear. Hover it to see exactly which slots.

Minimum Item Level
Type a number into Min iLvl and anyone under it is flagged with a raid warning and a sound. Summary tiles at the top count who is ready, who is below your minimum and who is offline.
